# Stranded Survival

Stranded Survival is a Roblox game script that creates a survival experience where players are stranded on an island. Their health bar decreases over time, and they must find food or fish to regain health. Every three days, a boat arrives with dangerous enemies who attack the players. The game features a day-night cycle, a speed boost button with a cooldown, and a health system that decreases over time.

## Install

1. Open Roblox Studio.
2. Create a new Baseplate or open an existing game.
3. In the Explorer, right-click on `ServerScriptService` and create a new `Script` named `BoatAttack`.
4. Copy the contents of `BoatAttack.lua` and paste them into the `BoatAttack` script.
5. Repeat steps 3 and 4 for `DayNightCycle`, `FishingSystem`, `FoodSystem`, and `PlayerHealth` scripts, placing them in `ServerScriptService`.
6. In the Explorer, right-click on `StarterPlayerScripts` and create a new `Script` named `SpeedButton`.
7. Copy the contents of `SpeedButton.lua` and paste them into the `SpeedButton` script.
8. Save your game.

## Use

1. Launch the game in Roblox Studio.
2. Players will spawn on the island with a health bar that decreases over time.
3. Players can find food to regain health or use a fishing rod to catch fish.
4. Every three days, a boat will arrive with dangerous enemies who will attack the players.
5. Players can use the speed boost button in the bottom center of their screen to temporarily increase their movement speed, but it has a cooldown.
